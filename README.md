## 🌱 What I'm Working On

Right now I'm focused on **making AI answers trustworthy when the source material disagrees with itself.**

My most recent project, [ParcelPilot](https://github.com/chandra-kp001/parcelpilot-ai-support), is built over a deliberately adversarial corpus — a deprecated policy sitting beside the current one with near-identical tables and different numbers, customer agreements that override the same SOP in opposite directions, and closed tickets containing answers a past agent got wrong. The work is in the parts that make that survivable:

- **Schema-constrained tool planning** so the model chooses actions, not free-form text
- **Deterministic calculators** for anything involving dates, SLAs or money
- **A verify pass** that rejects answers which aren't grounded in retrieved evidence
- **Permission-aware contexts** — one reasoning layer, genuinely different customer vs. internal access

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📦 [ParcelPilot — AI Support System](https://github.com/chandra-kp001/parcelpilot-ai-support)

A grounded AI support system for a B2B logistics platform, built over a **deliberately conflicting document corpus** — policies, SOPs, signed customer agreements and operational data that contradict each other on purpose. Two user contexts (customer-facing and internal operations) share one reasoning layer but hold genuinely different permissions. Schema-constrained tool planning, deterministic calculators, and a verify pass that rejects ungrounded answers. **349 tests.**

`FastAPI` · `LangGraph` · `PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector` · `sentence-transformers (BGE)` · `Groq` · `React 18` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind` · `Docker Compose` · `pytest`

---

### 🔬 [Production RAG System — CI-Gated Pipeline](https://github.com/chandra-kp001/production-RAG-system-with-CI-gated-pipeline)

A production-grade RAG system where **answer quality is enforced by CI**. A RAGAS evaluation gate runs on every push and pull request and fails the build when retrieval or faithfulness scores regress. Hybrid BM25 + vector retrieval with RRF fusion, cross-encoder reranking, and citation enforcement on generated answers.

`Python` · `LangChain` · `FAISS` · `rank-bm25` · `bge-reranker-base` · `Ollama (Llama 3)` · `RAGAS` · `GitHub Actions` · `FastAPI` · `Streamlit`

---

### 📚 [RAG System — "Ask My Docs"](https://github.com/chandra-kp001/RAG-System)

The interactive build of the same retrieval stack, aimed at exploration rather than CI. Turns raw PDFs, DOCX, TXT and Markdown into a searchable knowledge base and answers questions with source-backed citations through a Streamlit chat UI, with streaming responses and API readiness checks.

`FastAPI` · `Streamlit` · `FAISS` · `BM25 + RRF` · `BGE embeddings` · `Cross-encoder rerank` · `Ollama` · `RAGAS`

---

### 🎙️ [Athena — AI-Powered Speech Analysis](https://github.com/chandra-kp001/AI-powered-speech-analysis-system)

Analyses pitch presentations end to end: transcribes audio with Faster-Whisper, then measures speaking pace (WPM), pitch variation, volume and energy, pause and hesitation patterns, and filler words. A multi-agent layer returns feedback from four distinct investor perspectives — visionary, finance, skeptic and customer advocate — each landing on Invest / Not Invest / Need More Info.

`Python` · `Faster-Whisper` · `CTranslate2` · `librosa` · `Multi-agent LLM feedback`

---

### 😀 [Speech Emotion Recognition — Wav2Vec2](https://github.com/chandra-kp001/Speech-Emotion-Recognition-using-Wav2Vec2)

Emotion classification on speech audio by fine-tuning Wav2Vec2 on the RAVDESS dataset. Labels are parsed from the RAVDESS filename convention, audio is resampled to 16 kHz and padded to fixed length, and class imbalance is handled with stratified splits. Includes a custom PyTorch `Dataset`, waveform/spectrogram visualisation, and a demo app.

`PyTorch` · `Hugging Face Transformers` · `Wav2Vec2Processor` · `librosa` · `Jupyter`

---

### ✈️ [Flight Delay Analysis &amp; Prediction](https://github.com/chandra-kp001/air-travel-through-flight-delay-prediction)

Predicts both **whether** a flight will be delayed and **how long** the delay will last, using historical airline delay-cause data. Goes past accuracy into explainability: SHAP for model transparency, root-cause analysis across carrier, weather, NAS and late-aircraft factors, and an **Operational Adjustability Index** that ranks delays by how much an airline can actually control them.

`Python` · `scikit-learn` · `SHAP` · `pandas` · `Matplotlib` · `Jupyter`
